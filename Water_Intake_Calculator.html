How this is calculated & clinical notes

Baseline fluid needs are estimated at 33 mL per kg of body weight (0.5 fl oz/lb), within the standard 30–35 mL/kg adult guideline, plus 350 mL per 30 minutes of exercise and climate adjustments (+250 mL warm, +500 mL very hot). For comparison, the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (NASEM) sets adequate total daily water intake (from all beverages and food, where food typically supplies ~20%) at 3.7 L for men and 2.7 L for women. Pale-yellow urine is a practical everyday check for adequate hydration. Consult a healthcare provider if you have kidney, heart, or liver conditions requiring fluid limits.
